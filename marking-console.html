Trip hazard bank

Prepare trip hazards ahead of marking, from the marking schedule. Saved lists show up when you create a job, so the hazards are ready to click from the first paper. Seeding a job copies the list; editing it while marking never changes the bank.

Sync across devices

Syncs your whole state through one secret GitHub gist, so you can mark on your computer and phone. Create a token at github.com/settings/tokens (classic, scope gist), paste it below, then press Sync now. On a second device, paste the same token and the gist id.

New marking job

You mark one part across all students, then the next. Renaming a part keeps its marks; removing a part deletes its marks.

Import copies a list in, yours to edit; you can also add hazards as you mark. A bank list is one you prepared ahead; a live set is another job's working list.

Import trip hazards

Copies the hazards into this job; only ones not already here are added, and nothing you have logged is removed.
